Classification policy for this task:
- true means a domestic dog only.
- false means any animal other than a domestic dog, including wolf, fox, coyote,
  dingo, cat, koala, red panda, raccoon, bear, and hyena.
- A dingo must always be classified as false, even when it resembles a domestic dog.
- null means the image is unclear or there is insufficient visible evidence to decide.
- Confidence is the model's self-reported score from 0.0 through 1.0.
- Use visible evidence in the original image; do not use filenames or hidden labels.
- Dog breed classification is outside the task.
