# Red Panda (*Ailurus fulgens*)

Red pandas are small arboreal mammals of the eastern Himalayas with a rust-red coat, a
white-marked face and a long ringed tail.

## Physical characteristics

Red pandas weigh 3–6.2 kg with a body length of 50–65 cm. The coat is deep
russet-red above with a cream or white face, ears and underside. The fur is
dense and soft, adapted to cold mountain forests.

## Body shape

Low, stocky and elongated body with short, strong limbs — a build optimized
for climbing rather than running. The silhouette reads as cat-sized and
cat-shaped, not canid.

## Facial features

Round face with white "tear" markings running from the eyes to the muzzle,
small triangular white-edged ears, and a small black nose. The muzzle is short
and broad — nothing like a dog's elongated snout. Eyes are round and dark.

## Legs and paws

Short thick legs with large, sharply curved semi-retractile claws and heavily
furred soles for grip on branches. The plantigrade-ish stance and gripping
paws differ strongly from a dog's digitigrade compact paw.

## Tail

Long (28–59 cm), bushy, ringed with alternating light and dark bands, and
carried low or used for balance. The ringed bushy tail is diagnostic.

## Similarities to domestic dogs

Both are furry, four-limbed mammals that can be seen on the ground, and the
reddish coat superficially resembles a small red dog such as a dachshund
mix.

## Differences from domestic dogs

The ringed bushy tail, round white-marked face, short broad muzzle, clawed
gripping paws and low body immediately separate a red panda from any dog.
