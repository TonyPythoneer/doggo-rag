# Domestic Cat (*Felis catus*)

Cats are common non-dog animals that occasionally appear in ambiguous,
low-detail photographs alongside small dogs.

## Physical characteristics

Domestic cats weigh 3–7 kg typically, with a body length of 46–51 cm and
shoulder height 23–25 cm. Coats vary enormously in color and pattern. Cats are
digitigrade like dogs but substantially smaller and lighter framed.

## Body shape

Supple, flexible spine with a short, compact torso and relatively long hind
legs adapted for jumping. The silhouette is rounded and agile, with a
noticeably shorter muzzle and rounder head than any but the smallest dogs.

## Facial features

Short broad muzzle, round face, large forward-facing eyes with vertical-slit
pupils, and small triangular ears set wide. Whiskers are long and prominent.
The nose is small and triangular, and the face lacks a pronounced stop.

## Legs and paws

Retractile claws (a key difference from dogs), soft padded paws with five toes
on the forefeet. The stance is compact; cats walk with a direct-register gait.

## Tail

Long, flexible and cylindrical, carried upright or gently curved when
confident; it cannot be held in a dog-like sickle and is not bushy like a
fox's.

## Similarities to domestic dogs

Both are small domestic companion mammals with digitigrade locomotion, similar
coat color ranges, and forward-facing eyes. In blurred images, a small
long-bodied animal can read as a small dog.

## Differences from domestic dogs

Cats are smaller with a rounder head, much shorter muzzle, retractile claws,
vertical-slit pupils and a characteristic feline gait.
